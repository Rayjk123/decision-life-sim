A town that runs on decisions

Every sim here is a person with a job, a personality and money to manage. Each month some of them face a money decision: invest, save, buy a home or a rental, start a business, splurge, or retire. A decision model reads their situation and gives every legal option odds; then a seeded dice roll picks one, so the same town always replays the same way.

  1. Decision explorer: this month's decisions. Click one to see the odds, the dice roll and what the model saw.
  2. Leaderboard: click any sim to read their life story, money in and out, and what they own.
  3. Chart: how each personality type builds wealth over the years, in today's dollars.
